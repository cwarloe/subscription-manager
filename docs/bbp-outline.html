TITLE
Know What You Really Pay
A private ledger for subscription prices, student and military offers, bundles, and credit-card benefits.
ACT 1: SETUP
Setting Role Point A
You pay for many subscriptions, and cards, offers, and bundles can lower the real cost. You're the person who wants the lowest eligible out-of-pocket cost. Prices change, offers overlap, and a missing price is easy to mistake for zero.
TRANSITION 1
The ledger estimates what you pay without guessing. Three ideas explain it: what it tracks, how it keeps data honest, and the rules behind the math.
