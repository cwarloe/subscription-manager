TITLE
Know What You Really Pay
A private ledger for subscription prices, student and military offers, bundles, and credit-card benefits.
ACT 1: SETUP
Setting Role
You pay for many subscriptions, and cards, offers, and bundles can lower the real cost. You're the person who wants the lowest eligible out-of-pocket cost.
Point A (Where am I?) Call to Action (How do I get from A to B?) Point B (Where do I want to be?)
Prices change, offers overlap, and a missing price is easy to mistake for zero. Add your subscriptions and cards, refresh prices, and review the dashboard. A clear monthly picture of what you really pay.
